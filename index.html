The Story

Where I come from

A third culture kid with an immigrant background, shaped by growing up between cultures. I know how to adapt, observe, question and find my own place in unfamiliar environments. It's a blessing, a challenge, and sometimes a lifelong identity crisis.

The part you'd expect

I played by the playbook. Business school, good grades, scholarships, some of the most sought-after tech companies, a six-figure salary. I learned how to navigate the game and play it well.

The part you wouldn't expect

Nothing has shaped my raison d'être quite like grief. Having to accept the brutality of "never again" taught me how deeply the good and the bad can coexist. It changed my perspective on time, priorities and what it means to actually live. And it introduced me to a kind of loneliness I hadn't known before.

Where I am now

Back to the essentials of being human.

On the hunt for offline pleasures.

Reflecting on and rewiring limiting beliefs.

Balancing my business drive with my emotional cravings.

Embracing being a multipotentialite instead of squeezing myself into a niche.
