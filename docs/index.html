The Story

Meet Sinem

Raised between cultures, wired to adapt. A third-culture kid with an immigrant background and a work ethic that didn't exactly come out of nowhere. I've learned to read the room, find creative ways around obstacles and embrace uncertainty. A blessing, a challenge, and a lifelong identity crisis.

The part you'd expect

I played by the playbook. Business school, good grades, scholarships, some of the most sought-after tech companies, a six-figure salary. I knew how to play the game. And I played it well.

The part you wouldn't expect

Nothing has shaped my raison d'être quite like grief. Accepting the brutality of "never again" was my most painful lesson in impermanence. It made me realise that if something deserves your time, make it count. If it doesn't, walk away. Life is too short for anything in between.

It changed how I see time, priorities and where I choose to place my attention. If I leave a digital footprint, I'd like it to remind people to spend a little less time consuming life and a little more time actually living it.
